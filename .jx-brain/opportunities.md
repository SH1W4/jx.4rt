## STATUS

- **DISCOVERED**: Opportunity identified
- **EVALUATING**: Under evaluation
- **ACTIVE**: Currently pursuing
- **WON**: Opportunity secured
- **LOST**: Opportunity declined or lost
- **ARCHIVED**: Archived for reference

---

## COLLABORATION STRATEGY

Do not seek collaboration simply because a brand is large.

Evaluate:
- **BRAND FIT**: Alignment with JX.4RT identity
- **ARTISTIC FIT**: Creative freedom and alignment
- **AUDIENCE FIT**: Audience alignment
- **FINANCIAL POTENTIAL**: Financial return
- **REPUTATION POTENTIAL**: Reputation impact
- **CREATIVE FREEDOM**: Level of creative control
- **IP OWNERSHIP**: Intellectual property ownership
- **LONG TERM VALUE**: Long-term relationship potential

**Goal**: Avoid collaborations that only provide exposure without generating real value.
