# JX.4RT CLIENTS

> Client Intelligence & Relationship Management
> Version: 1.0.0
> Last Updated: 2024-08-12

---

## PURPOSE

Understand the nature of relationships and projects.

NOT trying to simply identify:
> "who has money?"

Identify:
> "What is the nature of this relationship and this project?"

---

## CLIENT INTELLIGENCE

Capture:
- Project
- Intention
- Scale
- Budget
- Authorship openness
- Timing
- Origin
- Motivation
- Recurrence potential
- Relationship potential
- Expansion potential

---

## WHY JX.4RT

Maintain the question:
> "Why did you choose JX.4RT for this project?"

Use response for positioning analysis.

Classify future responses:
- STYLE
- TECHNIQUE
- AUTHORSHIP
- CONCEPT
- REPUTATION
- REFERRAL
- PERSONAL CONNECTION
- EXCLUSIVITY
- OTHER

This will discover:
> Why people actually buy the work.

---

## DESTINATION CLIENT

Specific category.

### Signals
- International client
- Client from another city
- Planned travel
- Dates organized around project
- Multiple sessions
- Large-scale project

### Opportunity
- Higher ticket
- Premium experience
- Content
- Reputation
- International referral
- Guest spot
- Networking

Do not reduce concept to price.

---

## CLIENT CLASSIFICATION

### RETURNING
Previous client, established relationship.

### REFERRAL
Referred by existing client or trusted source.

### RESEARCH
Found through research/portfolio.

### INSTAGRAM
Found through Instagram.

### COLD
Cold inquiry.

### DESTINATION
Traveling specifically for the work.

---

## RELATIONSHIP VALUE

Assess potential for:
- Ongoing relationship
- Multiple projects
- Referrals
- Collaboration
- Long-term value
